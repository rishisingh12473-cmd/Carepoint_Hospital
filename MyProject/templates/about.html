Our approach

Listening is where good care begins.

CarePoint is a hospital-management website concept designed to make it easier for patients to explore services and request appointments. Our goal is to support respectful, coordinated care for individuals and families.

Our departments bring together a range of services, while our administrative team helps patients find the right next step. Appointment requests submitted online are reviewed by staff and require confirmation.
